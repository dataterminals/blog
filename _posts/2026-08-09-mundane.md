i feel like if there's anything more to the mundane... brainless... luxury
surveillance and mistreatment and debasement and disenfranchisement, the
mundane material excess cultural fatness, raw culturally obese-in-the-brain
schlock that describes the average texture of one's daily life as literally
any american, especially working class ones, then it only comes from just...
idk honest to god disruption. like nobody here is stumbling upon anything
profound by stating that you're a person in america who should never
underestimate their own need for convenience, it truly is disgusting, its like
people's consumptive tendencies here are rooted in nothing more but larping as
exploiters of others. like we can't do shit in life unless we have basically an
indentured servant do it for us, except now everyone can have one in a
never-ending chain of labor and fulfillment. so idk i dont feel very
fulfilled. i think one of the broader issues right now is that the obscenely
wealthy stopped feeling as if they needed to be classy and to contribute to
society; like most of them just feel like they don't owe us shit, even though
their entire life is basically on rent from the vast swath of working class
america. dudes like rockefeller and carnegie were shitheads by every stretch of
the imagination, sure, and they also donated wealth to at least idk. "bring art
to the masses". figuring that "well shit if I do nothing for these people what
happens when they come for me with the pitchforks" and now we've just
imprisoned ourselves in a pillow-fort, non-exitable, where it doesn't matter
what you think, because you can't do shit about it, and we've already marketed
a reality to you where resisting against that is just another, fractal level of
consumption itself. welcome to the loop
